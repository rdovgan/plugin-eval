# Cancellation and refund rules

Owner: product team. Last reviewed: 2026-06-12.

The refund depends on the time between the cancellation and the check-in time of the booking:

| Time before check-in | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| 48 hours or more | 100% of the booking total |
| 24 to 48 hours | 50% |
| less than 24 hours | none |

The partner the booking came from is notified about every cancellation with the refund amount.

Partial stays (a guest leaves early) are not covered by these rules; they are handled manually by support.
